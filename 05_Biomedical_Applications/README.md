# 05 — Biomedical Applications

## Research directions
Piezoelectric materials are investigated for mechanical-to-electrical stimulation, bone/tissue regeneration, cell–material interactions, wearable/implantable sensing, ultrasound transduction, and therapeutic systems coupling mechanical and electrical effects. These are distinct application classes: a measured piezoelectric response alone does not establish biocompatibility, therapeutic efficacy, or safety.

## Material considerations
- PVDF-based polymers: flexible and processable; phase composition, crystallinity, and poling matter.
- BaTiO3 and other lead-free ceramics: useful electromechanical properties, but particle release and dissolution need assessment.
- Ceramic–polymer composites: tune flexibility and response; filler dispersion and interfaces are critical.
- Bio-derived materials: may offer favorable biological interfaces, but properties can vary.
- Implantable systems: require sterilization compatibility, fatigue resistance, encapsulation, and long-term stability.
- Lead-containing materials require particular attention to exposure, leaching, manufacturing controls, and end-of-life management.

## Biological validation ladder
1. Physicochemical characterization and extract/leachate testing.
2. Cytocompatibility using relevant cell types and controls.
3. Dose- and time-dependent testing rather than a single condition.
4. Inflammatory response and application-specific functional endpoints.
5. Mechanistic tests separating electrical stimulation from material chemistry, loading, and heating.
6. Justified in vivo studies with ethics approval and appropriate reporting.

Use positive/negative controls, independent replicates, predefined analysis, and transparent reporting. Cell-culture data alone do not establish clinical readiness.

## Tissue-engineering questions
- Is electrical output measured under biologically relevant loading?
- Does the scaffold preserve pore architecture and mechanical properties?
- Are outcomes due to stimulation, topography, stiffness, chemistry, or released ions?
- Is performance reproducible under physiological hydration?
- Does sterilization change phase, surface chemistry, or response?

This file is a topic framework, not medical advice or evidence of clinical effectiveness.
