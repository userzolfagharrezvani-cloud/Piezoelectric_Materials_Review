# Recent Review Articles on Piezoelectric Materials (2022–2026)
 
**Publisher-focused selection:** Nature Portfolio, ACS, Elsevier, and Wiley. Records below were located on publisher pages and are categorized as reviews where the publisher labels them as such. The requested window extends to 2027, but as of 15 October 2026, 2027 publications are future-dated and are not included. This is a selected bibliography, not an exhaustive systematic review.

## Nature Portfolio

1. Lee, G. et al. “Piezoelectric energy harvesting using mechanical metamaterials and phononic crystals.” *Communications Physics* 5, 94 (2022). **Review Article; open access.**  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s42005-022-00869-4  
   Focus: mechanical metamaterials and phononic crystals for localizing, focusing, and guiding elastic/acoustic waves to improve piezoelectric energy harvesting.

2. Chen, B. et al. “Flexible piezoelectrics: integration of sensing, actuating and energy harvesting.” *npj Flexible Electronics* 9, 58 (2025). **Review; open access.**  
   Publisher page: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41528-025-00432-5  
   Focus: flexible piezoelectric materials and integrated sensing, actuation, and energy-harvesting systems.

## American Chemical Society (ACS)

3. He, Q.; Briscoe, J. “Piezoelectric Energy Harvester Technologies: Synthesis, Mechanisms, and Multifunctional Applications.” *ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces* (2024). **Review; open access.**  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.3c17037  
   Focus: synthesis, mechanisms, device structures, and multifunctional energy-harvesting applications.

4. Deng, Y. et al. “Recent Advances in Piezoelectric Coupled with Photocatalytic Reaction System: Synergistic Mechanism, Enhancement Factors, and Application.” *ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces* 16 (2024). **Review.**  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.4c03256  
   Focus: piezo-photocatalysis, synergistic mechanisms, enhancement factors, and applications.

5. Zhang, Z. et al. “Design and Manufacturing of Piezoelectric Biomaterials for Bioelectronics and Biomedical Applications.” *Chemical Reviews* (2025). **Review; open access.**  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemrev.5c00399  
   Focus: piezoelectric biomaterials, molecular/material design, manufacturing, bioelectronics, and biomedical applications.

6. Singh, D. et al. “Piezoelectric Energy Harvesting for Pacemaker Applications: Current State-of-the-Art, Materials, Design, and Alternative Technologies.” *ACS Biomaterials Science & Engineering* (2025). **Review.**  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1021/acsbiomaterials.5c00298  
   Focus: implantable pacemakers, biomechanical energy harvesting, materials, and device design.

## Elsevier

7. “High-electromechanical performance for high-power piezoelectric applications: Fundamental, progress, and perspective.” *Progress in Materials Science* 127 (2022), 100944. **Review.**  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pmatsci.2022.100944  
   Focus: high-power piezoelectric materials, electromechanical quality factor, coupling parameters, and temperature stability.

8. “Atomic layer deposition of piezoelectric materials: a timely review.” *Materials Today Energy* 39 (2024), 101457. **Review; open access.**  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mtener.2023.101457  
   Focus: atomic-layer deposition of ultrathin piezoelectric films, thickness/composition control, transition-metal dichalcogenides, and metal oxides.

9. Pan, X. et al. “Mechanical energy harvesting based on the piezoelectric materials: Recent advances and future perspectives.” *Chemical Engineering Journal* 497 (2024), 154249. **Review.**  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cej.2024.154249  
   Focus: inorganic, organic, and hybrid materials; sensors, medical diagnostics, energy harvesting, electrode selection, and machine learning for materials discovery.

10. “Deployment of piezoelectric transducers for diverse technical applications: A comprehensive review.” *Results in Engineering* (2024), 103881. **Review; open access.**  
    DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rineng.2024.103881  
    Focus: ceramics, polymers, single crystals, and composites, with energy harvesting, sound absorption, water treatment, structural health monitoring, and biomedical applications.

11. “Recent development in piezoelectric materials and devices for cryogenic environments.” *Sensors and Actuators A: Physical* 386 (2025), 116317. **Review.**  
    DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sna.2025.116317  
    Focus: piezoelectric materials and devices operating below 120 K, including low-temperature performance challenges.

12. Iwuozor, K. O. et al. “Polymer-based piezoelectric materials: Structure, mechanism, applications, and future trends.” *Surfaces and Interfaces* 70 (2025), 106855. **Review.**  
    DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.surfin.2025.106855  
    Focus: polymer piezoelectricity, dipole alignment, PVDF and related polymers, synthesis, properties, and applications.

13. “Advances in piezoelectric energy harvesting: Materials, configurations, and power optimisation strategies.” *Results in Engineering* (2025), 107782. **Review; open access.**  
    DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rineng.2025.107782  
    Focus: power density, harvester architectures, flexible versus ceramic devices, and optimization strategies.

14. “Piezoelectric enables Universality Enhancement of conventional water Treatment: Mechanisms, Effects, and challenges.” *Chemical Engineering Journal* 510 (2025), 161704. **Review.**  
    DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cej.2025.161704  
    Focus: piezo-assisted water treatment, piezocatalysis, pollutant transformation, self-cleaning membranes, and antifouling.

15. “A critical review on piezoelectric supercapacitors: Fundamentals, recent advances, and future directions.” *Journal of Alloys and Compounds* 1024 (2025), 180169. **Review.**  
    DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jallcom.2025.180169  
    Focus: integration of piezoelectricity and supercapacitors, mechanisms, current progress, and research challenges.

16. “Advances in piezotronics and piezo-phototronics of two-dimensional semiconductor materials.” *Chip* 4(2) (2025), 100131. **Review; open access.**  
    DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chip.2025.100131  
    Focus: 2D semiconductor materials, strain-induced polarization, piezotronics, and piezo-phototronics.

## Bibliographic quality notes

- The publisher pages are the primary records; follow the DOI to verify the final author list, issue/page/article number, and online-first versus issue year before citing.
- Items 2026–2027 are not asserted here unless independently confirmed from a publisher record. No 2027 papers are listed because 2027 is still in the future as of 15 October 2026.
- Some entries have abbreviated author lists because this is a discovery register; export the full citation from the publisher for manuscript use.
- This is a targeted publisher selection, not proof that every relevant review from these publishers has been captured.
