## Verification protocol
1. Resolve each DOI at https://doi.org/.
2. Confirm title, complete author list, journal, year, volume, pages/article number, and article type.
3. Confirm that the paper is a review if it is counted as one; a “Feature Article” can have a review-like scope but should retain the publisher's article-type label.
4. Save the publisher's RIS/BibTeX export for manuscript work.
5. Do not cite a search-result snippet instead of reading the paper.
6. For systematic reviews, log database, exact query, search date, result count, and screening decisions.

Use the target journal's required citation style. The abbreviated records here are a working register, not final submission-ready references.
