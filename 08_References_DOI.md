## Verification protocol
1. Resolve the DOI at https://doi.org/.
2. Confirm title, authors, journal, year, volume, pages/article number, and article type.
3. Confirm the work is a review if it is counted as one.
4. Save RIS/BibTeX citation export for manuscript work.
5. Do not cite a search-result snippet instead of reading the paper.
6. For systematic reviews, log database, exact query, search date, result count, and screening decisions.

Use the target journal's required citation style. The short references in this repository are not submission-ready full bibliographic entries.
