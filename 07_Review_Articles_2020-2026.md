# Review Articles on Piezoelectric Materials and Applications (2020–2026)

This is a curated starting list of reviews with DOI/publisher records. It is not an exhaustive systematic review. Confirm citation exports and publication metadata before manuscript submission.

## Broad materials and applications
1. Mahapatra, S. D. et al. “Piezoelectric Materials for Energy Harvesting and Sensing Applications: Roadmap for Future Smart Materials.” Advanced Science (2021). Review. https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202100864  
   Scope: nanostructures, polymers, composites, films, harvesting, and sensing.
2. Sekhar, M. C. et al. “A Review on Piezoelectric Materials and Their Applications.” Crystal Research and Technology (2023). Review. https://doi.org/10.1002/crat.202200130  
   Scope: lead-based and lead-free materials; catalysis, harvesting, sensors, biomedical engineering, and additive manufacturing.
3. Wang, Z. et al. “Applications of Piezoelectric Materials in Biomedical Engineering.” Macromolecular Bioscience 25 (2025), e2500033. Review. https://doi.org/10.1002/mabi.202500033  
   Scope: biosensing, energy harvesting, tissue engineering, implants, and disease-treatment research. Publisher record confirms first publication on 28 April 2025.

## Energy harvesting
4. Sezer, N.; Koç, M. “A comprehensive review on the state-of-the-art of piezoelectric energy harvesting.” Nano Energy 80 (2021), 105567. Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nanoen.2020.105567  
   Scope: principles, materials, device structures, and applications across scales.
5. Ghazanfarian, J.; Mohammadi, M. M.; Uchino, K. “Piezoelectric Energy Harvesting: A Systematic Review of Reviews.” Actuators 10(12) (2021), 312. Review of reviews. https://doi.org/10.3390/act10120312  
   Scope: synthesizes review literature on materials, models, device designs, and applications.
6. Li, T. et al. “Piezoelectric Energy Harvesting Technology: From Materials, Structures, to Applications.” Small Structures (2022). Review. https://doi.org/10.1002/sstr.202100128  
   Scope: performance parameters and links between materials, architectures, and applications.
7. Brusa, E.; Carrera, A.; Delprete, C. “A Review of Piezoelectric Energy Harvesting: Materials, Design, and Readout Circuits.” Actuators 12 (2023), 457. Review. https://doi.org/10.3390/act12120457  
   Scope: materials, device design, and readout/power electronics.
8. Radeef, Z. S. et al. “Advances in piezoelectric energy harvesting: Materials, configurations, and power optimisation strategies.” Results in Engineering 28 (2025), 107782. Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rineng.2025.107782  
   Scope: material classes, device configurations, normalized power density, and optimization strategies.

## Flexible and wearable electronics
9. Chen, Y.; Zhang, X.; Lu, C. “Flexible piezoelectric materials and strain sensors for wearable electronics and artificial intelligence applications.” Chemical Science 15 (2024), 16436–16466. Review. https://doi.org/10.1039/D4SC05166A  
   Scope: flexible materials, strain sensors, wearable devices, electronic skin, and AI-enabled applications.
10. “Piezoelectric materials for flexible and wearable electronics: A review.” Materials & Design. Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.matdes.2021.110164  
    Scope: flexible/stretchable inorganic, polymeric, and bio-piezoelectric materials and physiological sensing. Check publisher metadata for final year and author formatting.

## Environmental piezocatalysis
11. Liu, J. et al. “Piezocatalytic Techniques in Environmental Remediation.” Angewandte Chemie International Edition 62 (2023), e202213927. Review. https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202213927  
    Scope: mechanisms, material classes, pollutant remediation, challenges, and outlook.
12. Alshammari, K. F. “Recent advances of piezo-catalysis and photocatalysis for efficient environmental remediation.” Luminescence 39 (2024), e4808. Review. https://doi.org/10.1002/bio.4808  
    Scope: combined piezocatalysis/photocatalysis for environmental remediation.

## Search priorities to expand the collection
- Lead-free piezoelectric ceramics and high-temperature piezoelectrics.
- Piezoelectric cellulose, bio-derived materials, and nanofibers.
- Actuators, ultrasonic transducers, MEMS/NEMS.
- Piezoelectric biomedical materials and tissue engineering.
- Piezocatalysis and piezo-photocatalysis, including realistic water matrices.
- New review papers published during 2026.

## Screening note
This list is not yet a complete evidence map for all applications or all publications through 2026. A systematic review should define databases, search date, exact query, inclusion/exclusion criteria, deduplication, and screening process. Check publisher records and citation exports before citation.
