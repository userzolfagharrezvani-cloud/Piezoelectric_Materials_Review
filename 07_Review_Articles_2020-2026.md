# Review Articles on Piezoelectric Materials and Applications (2020–2026)

This is a verified starter list of reviews with publisher pages and DOI links checked during preparation. It is not an exhaustive systematic review. Confirm citation exports and publication metadata before manuscript submission.

## Broad materials and applications
1. Mahapatra, S. D. et al. “Piezoelectric Materials for Energy Harvesting and Sensing Applications: Roadmap for Future Smart Materials.” Advanced Science (2021). Review. https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202100864  
   Scope: nanostructures, polymers, composites, films, harvesting, and sensing.
2. Sekhar, M. C. et al. “A Review on Piezoelectric Materials and Their Applications.” Crystal Research and Technology (2023). Review. https://doi.org/10.1002/crat.202200130  
   Scope: lead-based and lead-free materials; catalysis, harvesting, sensors, biomedical engineering, and additive manufacturing.

## Energy harvesting
3. Sezer, N.; Koç, M. “A comprehensive review on the state-of-the-art of piezoelectric energy harvesting.” Nano Energy 80 (2021), 105567. Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nanoen.2020.105567  
   Scope: principles, materials, device structures, and applications across scales.
4. Li, T. et al. “Piezoelectric Energy Harvesting Technology: From Materials, Structures, to Applications.” Small Structures (2022). Review. https://doi.org/10.1002/sstr.202100128  
   Scope: performance parameters and links between materials, architectures, and applications.
5. Brusa, E.; Carrera, A.; Delprete, C. “A Review of Piezoelectric Energy Harvesting: Materials, Design, and Readout Circuits.” Actuators 12 (2023), 457. Review. https://doi.org/10.3390/act12120457  
   Scope: materials, device design, and readout/power electronics.

## Flexible and wearable electronics
6. Chen, Y.; Zhang, X.; Lu, C. “Flexible piezoelectric materials and strain sensors for wearable electronics and artificial intelligence applications.” Chemical Science 15 (2024), 16436–16466. Review. https://doi.org/10.1039/D4SC05166A  
   Scope: flexible materials, strain sensors, wearable devices, electronic skin, and AI-enabled applications.
7. “Piezoelectric materials for flexible and wearable electronics: A review.” Materials & Design. Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.matdes.2021.110164  
   Scope: flexible/stretchable inorganic, polymeric, and bio-piezoelectric materials and physiological sensing. Check publisher metadata for final year and author formatting.

## Environmental piezocatalysis
8. Liu, J. et al. “Piezocatalytic Techniques in Environmental Remediation.” Angewandte Chemie International Edition 62 (2023), e202213927. Review. https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202213927  
   Scope: mechanisms, material classes, pollutant remediation, challenges, and outlook.
9. Alshammari, K. F. “Recent advances of piezo-catalysis and photocatalysis for efficient environmental remediation.” Luminescence 39 (2024), e4808. Review. https://doi.org/10.1002/bio.4808  
   Scope: combined piezocatalysis/photocatalysis for environmental remediation.

## Screening note
This list does not yet constitute a complete evidence map for biomedical devices, actuators/ultrasonics, lead-free ceramics, or every year through 2026. A systematic review should define databases, search date, exact query, inclusion/exclusion criteria, deduplication, and screening process. Check academic discovery results against publisher records before citation.
