# Review Articles on Piezoelectric Materials and Applications (2020–2026)

Curated starter list with DOI/publisher records. This is not an exhaustive systematic review. Confirm citation exports and publication metadata before manuscript submission. Items published in 2026 are included when the publisher record was available by 13 October 2026.

## Broad materials and applications
1. Mahapatra, S. D. et al. “Piezoelectric Materials for Energy Harvesting and Sensing Applications: Roadmap for Future Smart Materials.” *Advanced Science* (2021). Review. https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202100864  
   Scope: nanostructures, polymers, composites, films, harvesting, and sensing.
2. Sekhar, M. C. et al. “A Review on Piezoelectric Materials and Their Applications.” *Crystal Research and Technology* (2023). Review. https://doi.org/10.1002/crat.202200130  
   Scope: lead-based and lead-free materials; catalysis, harvesting, sensors, biomedical engineering, and additive manufacturing.

## Priority areas for further searching
- Lead-free and high-temperature piezoelectric ceramics.
- Piezoelectric polymers, cellulose, bio-derived materials, and nanofibers.
- Actuators, ultrasonic transducers, MEMS/NEMS.
- Flexible sensors and wearable systems.
- Piezocatalysis under realistic water matrices and low-frequency mechanical excitation.
- Review papers published in 2026 in every application category.

## Screening note
This list is not a complete evidence map for every application or publication through 2026. A systematic review should define databases, search date, exact query, inclusion/exclusion criteria, deduplication, and screening. Publisher records should be checked before formal citation.
