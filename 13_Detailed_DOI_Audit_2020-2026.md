**Audit date:** 13 October 2026  
**Scope:** DOI records previously listed in this repository and selected specialist reviews added during the October 2026 update. Publisher landing pages were prioritized; PubMed/PMC or DOAJ was used as a cross-check where useful.

## How to interpret status

- **Verified — publisher record:** DOI resolves to a publisher page and title/author/journal/year/article type were checked against the available record.
- **Verified — cross-check:** publisher record was supplemented by a bibliographic database record.
- **Needs follow-up:** the DOI/title combination could not be independently confirmed from the accessible records during this pass. Do not cite it until checked manually.
- Publication year below follows the version-of-record issue where visible. When online-first and issue years differ, both are stated.

## Consolidation and citation rules

1. Rows 6 and 29, and rows 7 and 30, are duplicate records and should be counted only once in any review-paper totals.
2. A DOI that resolves is not, by itself, proof that the title, authors, publication year, or review classification previously recorded are correct.
3. Distinguish issue year from first-online year when they differ.
4. Keep formal publisher labels such as “Review”, “Review Article”, and “Feature Article” rather than reclassifying articles based only on their abstracts.
5. This is a verified curated bibliography, **not** a completed systematic review. Do not report PRISMA counts or claim exhaustive coverage unless a reproducible database search and screening log are completed.
6. For manuscript submission, export the citation from each publisher's “Cite / Download citation” function and compare it with this matrix. Use the full article text to determine the exact applications and limitations covered.
