## Research synthesis and critical appraisal

Across these reviews, the recurring concept is mechanically activated generation of local electrical cues that may influence cell adhesion, proliferation, differentiation and tissue repair. The evidence base spans different material classes and experimental models, so findings should not be treated as directly comparable without checking composition, piezoelectric coefficient, poling, geometry, mechanical loading, output under hydrated physiological conditions, cell model, controls and endpoint definitions.

Important translation questions include:
- Does the material generate reproducible signals under realistic physiological loads?
- Are observed biological effects attributable to electrical stimulation rather than surface chemistry, released ions, stiffness, topography, heating or reactive oxygen species?
- Have inflammatory response, degradation products, sterilization, fatigue and long-term biosafety been assessed?
- For therapeutic claims, are in vivo efficacy and safety supported, or is evidence limited to cell culture or animal models?
- For self-powered sensors, are sensitivity, selectivity, drift, calibration and real-world validation reported?

## Bibliographic verification status

Consensus records provide searchable bibliographic summaries, but they do not replace the publisher's version of record. DOI fields were not available for every result in the search response. Do **not** infer missing DOIs from article titles. Before formal citation, open each record and publisher page, verify DOI, author spelling, article type, volume/issue/article number and online-first versus issue year.
