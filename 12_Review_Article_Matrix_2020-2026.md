## How to use the matrix
1. Select one application column before beginning a focused review.
2. Open the DOI record and export the publisher citation; do not rely on abbreviated entries here for final manuscripts.
3. Read the abstract and full text where available, then record materials covered, methods of synthesis/measurement, key conclusions, and limitations.
4. Follow the review's references and citing articles to identify primary research.
5. Track duplicate coverage: a broad review and a specialist review may discuss the same primary studies.
6. Record excluded papers and the reason for exclusion if this becomes a systematic review.

## Important limitations
- This matrix is selective and should not be described as exhaustive.
- Some articles have online-first dates in one year and issue assignment in the next; preserve both fields in the final database.
- “Review”, “systematic review”, “review of reviews”, and “feature article” are not interchangeable. Record the publisher's article type.
- For a formal systematic review, run the documented database searches, record actual result counts and dates, and complete independent screening where feasible.
